Furniture FAQ

Questions furniture makers ask first.

Can it handle custom dimensions, not just standard sizes?

Yes. Dimensions are first-class characteristics — define width, depth, height in millimeters or inches, with min/max bounds per product. Your pricing formula can use those values directly for per-cubic-meter material costs, perimeter-based piping, or any custom rule. The customer types a number, the price updates instantly.

Can configurations show estimated lead times?

Yes. Lead time can be a derived field in the same formula engine that computes price. Show "4–6 weeks" for in-stock fabrics, "8–10 weeks" for special orders, and surface the estimate directly on the configurator and on the inquiry that lands in your inbox.

Can I show fabric swatches, not just images?

Yes. Every characteristic value can have its own swatch image, label, and price modifier. Customers see real fabric textures in the picker, the configured product image swaps to match, and the per-square-meter fabric cost flows into the price automatically.

Can customers save and share their configurations?

Yes. Every configuration has a sharable URL — copy it to send to a partner, send to a customer to review, or save for later. The inquiry submission includes the same link, so your sales team can re-open exactly what the customer saw.

Can dealers and showrooms have their own pricing?

Yes. Each tenant or sub-tenant can have its own price book, currency, and branded PDF template. Dealers see dealer pricing; the public site shows retail. Same products, same formulas, different multipliers.
