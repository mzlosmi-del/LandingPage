The problem

Signs are quoted by hand —
and that's where the margin dies.

If you cut, print, or fabricate custom signage, four problems quietly eat your day every single week.

Substrate questions become email threads

Foamex vs Dibond vs aluminum vs acrylic — each one prices differently per square meter and behaves differently outdoors. Customers email "how much for…?", and your designer answers instead of designing.

Custom dimensions break standard pricing

No two jobs are the same size. Per-job pricing is manual; per-square-meter pricing needs a formula. Spreadsheets help, until they don't.

Installation zones vary, but the website doesn't

Within-city, suburb, rural — different drive time, different rate. Your team has to ask "where are you?" before they can quote, and most customers won't answer until they see a price.

Signage FAQ

Questions sign shops ask first.

Can customers upload their own artwork files?

Yes. Add a file-upload field as a characteristic on the product. Customers can attach AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, or any format you accept, with size limits you control. The file is stored with the inquiry and surfaces alongside the configuration and price in your dashboard.

Can we let customers type text and pick fonts in the configurator?

Yes. Free-text characteristics let customers enter sign copy, and a dropdown of fonts lets them pick a typeface. The text length can flow into your pricing formula — for example, per-character or per-line surcharges for vinyl cutting. Final artwork can be auto-rendered for the inquiry preview.

Does it support a proof workflow before production?

Yes. Each inquiry has a status workflow you can extend — new, proofing, approved, in production, shipped. Send the PDF quote as the initial proof, then use the dashboard to track approvals. Customers can be looped in via email link.

Can we vary installation pricing by zone?

Yes. Add an installation characteristic with zones based on postal code, region, or distance bands. The formula engine applies the right surcharge automatically and shows the customer the installed total. The line item appears separately on the PDF quote.

Can we surface a rush-order surcharge?

Yes. Add a "lead time" characteristic with options like Standard, Express, Rush. Each option can apply its own percentage uplift or flat fee, and any option can hide other options that don't qualify. The selected lead time and surcharge travel with the inquiry and print on the PDF quote.
