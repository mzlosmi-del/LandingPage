Dimension by dimension

Where the two approaches really differ.

Enterprise CPQ platforms and a modern embeddable configurator solve overlapping problems, but they're built for very different teams. Here's what changes on each axis.

Time to live

Enterprise CPQ rollouts are measured in months — discovery, scoping, modeling, integration, training, rollout. Configureout is measured in afternoons. Define a product, build a formula, paste the embed code on your existing site. You can ship a working configurator on one product line before lunch and add the rest as you go.

Total cost of ownership

Legacy platforms bundle a high implementation cost (€40k–€200k+ in consulting), an annual contract for the platform itself, and per-seat charges that climb every time you grow the team. Configureout is usage-based: you pay per inquiry received — no setup fees, no seat licences, no fixed tiers, no upfront commitment. Costs scale with the demand the configurator brings you, and a quiet month costs you nothing.

Implementation complexity

Enterprise CPQ is a consulting project. You'll need a partner, a steering committee, and a multi-quarter project plan. Configureout is self-serve: define characteristics, build a formula in the visual editor, embed the widget. If you outgrow self-serve, we offer a paid onboarding service — but it's an option, not a requirement.

Who it's for

Enterprise CPQ platforms are built for Fortune-500 manufacturing operations with global sales teams, ERP integrations, and dedicated CPQ admins. Configureout is built for SMB and mid-market manufacturers, custom shops, and product brands that want a configurator on their site without becoming a CPQ shop themselves.
