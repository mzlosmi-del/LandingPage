The trap

The contact-form trap.

A contact form looks like the obvious first step for a configurable product. It's also where most of your interested buyers quietly disappear.

Visitors don't trust "we'll get back to you within 24 hours"

By the time you reply, they've already pinged three competitors who all promise the same thing. The first usable price wins, and your form was never in the running.

Your inbox fills with underspecified requests you can't quote

"Hi, how much for a sofa?" "I need a window, can you send a price?" Every reply starts a clarifying email thread that adds days before a real number reaches the customer.

Your team wastes hours quoting prospects who'd never have bought

Without a price filter on the page, every curious browser becomes a "manual quote." Your best sales people spend their day estimating jobs for visitors who weren't going to convert anyway.
