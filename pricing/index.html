Billing FAQ

Questions about plans & billing.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free tier requires no credit card. You can build a configurator, embed it, and start collecting inquiries without entering any payment details. You only add a card when you upgrade to a paid plan.

What's a configuration vs an inquiry?

A configuration is anytime a visitor opens the widget and starts picking options. An inquiry is when they finish, fill in their contact details, and submit. Configurations are essentially unlimited on every paid plan; inquiries are the metered metric, because that's what creates real value for you.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any point from the dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing is available on Starter, Growth, and Scale with two months free (about 17% off). Contact us to switch your existing subscription to annual.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

We don't cut off your widget. If you approach your monthly inquiry limit, we email you a heads-up and suggest an upgrade. If you go over, additional inquiries are billed at a per-inquiry overage rate at the end of the cycle.

Is there a free trial of paid plans?

The Free tier is effectively an open-ended trial of the core engine. For Starter, Growth, and Scale features specifically, book a consultation and we'll set you up with a 14-day trial on a real product.

Do you offer refunds?

If something goes wrong in your first 30 days on a paid plan, email us and we'll refund the unused portion. Outside that window we don't typically refund past months, but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges.

Is there a setup fee?

No mandatory setup fee. The Free, Starter, Growth, and Scale plans all start immediately. For teams that want help, we offer optional paid Setup sprints from €500.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your billing settings. Annual prepay is non-refundable but you keep access until the period ends.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your tenant is downgraded to Free. Existing products and inquiries are retained for 90 days, after which they're permanently deleted. You can export everything via the Settings page or our REST API at any time.

Do you have a free trial of Growth?

Yes — the 60-day pilot gives you Growth-tier features for 60 days, paired with a €500 Quick Setup sprint (refundable if you don't convert).
